Usage Limits

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Obliger is proudly free to download and free to use. In order to keep the cost of running Obliger's servers manageable as an independent micro-app, your account has the following limits:

Current limits

  • You can own up to 3 Circles.
  • You can belong to up to 5 Circles total, including ones you own.
  • A Circle can have up to 10 members.

If these limits are getting in the way of how you'd like to use Obliger, contact us at contact@obligerapp.com, we might be able to make an exception for devoted users.

If Obliger ever grows enough that running it for free stops being sustainable, we may introduce a paid option in the future. If that happens, this page will be updated well before anything changes.
